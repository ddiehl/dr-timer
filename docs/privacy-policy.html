Privacy Policy

Pilot Timer for iPhone · Effective 28 October 2026

Pilot Timer collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network requests of any kind. Nothing you enter leaves your device.

What the app stores

Pilot Timer saves the flights you plan — a flight name, and for each leg a landmark name and a time — along with the state of a run in progress, so that a flight resumes correctly if the app is closed mid-leg. This is written as ordinary files inside the app's own storage area on your iPhone.

This information is never transmitted anywhere. We cannot see it, and we have no server that could receive it.

Device backups

Because these files live in the app's documents area, they are included in your iPhone's own backups if you have iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups switched on. Those backups belong to you and are governed by Apple's terms, not by us. We have no access to them.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email collection
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage measurement
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers, or tracking across apps
  • No location access
  • No contacts, photos, calendar, or health access
  • No third-party SDKs or external code libraries
  • No network connections, so no data is sent or received

Notifications and sound

If you allow notifications, Pilot Timer uses them only to sound the alert when a leg's time is up. These notifications are created and delivered on your device. They are not push notifications, no server is involved, and no device token is collected. The app also plays audio and vibrates for the same alert, and monitors your volume level locally so it can warn you when the alert would be too quiet to hear.

Information Apple receives

Downloading the app from the App Store is a transaction between you and Apple, and Apple may provide us with aggregate statistics such as download counts, and crash reports if you have chosen to share them. This data is anonymous and is not linked to you. It is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Pilot Timer is a tool for flight training and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

Deleting your data

Deleting a flight in the app removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored. Since nothing is ever sent to us, there is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to make.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here and the effective date above will change. If a future version ever collects or transmits data, that will be stated plainly before it does.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the app itself, are welcome at daniel@spartatechnology.com.

Pilot Timer is a timing aid for dead-reckoning practice. It is not a navigation system and is not certified for flight.
